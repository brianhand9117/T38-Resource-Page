Pin Color Reference

Airport Inclusion Criteria

  • Must be in CONUS with a valid ICAO identifier (K___)
  • Longest declared LDA must be ≥ 7,000 ft
  • Must have government contract fuel on file

Pin Colors

  • Green Pushpin Recently landed by a T-38 crew (no issues flagged), or airport is on the Whitelist.
  • Blue Pushpin JASU listed in the A/FD but no recent T-38 ops. Call FBO to verify cart availability.
  • Yellow Pushpin No JASU listed in the A/FD and no recent T-38 ops. Verify start-cart availability.
  • Red Diamond (Category 2 / 3) Requires extra planning and/or approval before use.
  • Red Circle (Category 1) T-38 operations are prohibited.
  • Red Pushpin (Blacklisted) T-38 operations are not authorized.
